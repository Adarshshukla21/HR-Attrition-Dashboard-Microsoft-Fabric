# 📊HR Attrition Analysis Workforce Insights

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Microsoft Fabric and Power BI. The dashboard helps understand employee turnover patterns and explore factors that may influence employees leaving an organization.

The report contains two interactive pages: Overview and Deep Dive.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Fabric
- Power BI
- Semantic Model
- HR Analytics Dataset
- Data Visualization

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Headcount | 1,470 |
| Employees Left | 237 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.1% |
| Average Monthly Income | 6,503 |
| Average Tenure of Leavers | 5.1 Years |

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview
The Overview page provides a summary of employee attrition through:

- Overall Attrition Rate
- Total Employees and Employees Left
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Salary Band
- Attrition Decomposition Analysis
- Interactive Department, Gender, and Salary Band Filters

### 2. Deep Dive
The Deep Dive page provides detailed analysis of employee attrition based on:

- Age Group
- Gender
- Education Field
- Marital Status
- Business Travel
- Overtime
- Interactive Filters for deeper analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The overall employee attrition rate is 16.1%.
- A total of 237 employees have left the organization.
- The Sales department shows the highest attrition rate among the departments displayed.
- Sales Representatives have a comparatively high attrition rate.
- Employees in the Under 3k salary band show higher attrition than other salary bands displayed.
- The 18–25 age group shows a relatively high attrition rate.
- Overtime, marital status, education field, and business travel can be explored to understand workforce patterns.
